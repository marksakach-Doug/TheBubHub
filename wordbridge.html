    Play without a screen

    You need a dictionary, some paper, and a little patience.

    1. Open the dictionary and find a word that is both a noun and a verb and has several meanings. Bank, pitch and cover would all do.
    2. Pick two meanings that feel far apart. Read them aloud.
    3. Everyone writes one word that fits both meanings, folds the paper, and waits.
    4. Open them together. The group decides the bridges (or take turns being the arbiter). Words that are the same across players are usually bridges.
    5. The person on the left chooses the next word.
    6. If you want to add depth, write an explanation sentence along with the words. Read the explanation first.
