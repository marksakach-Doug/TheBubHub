Phrase Builder — intro/outro pairs

An intro and an outro always ship as one pair. The game deals a whole pair, never a random intro + random outro. Tempo, swing, scale and timbre are shared so the two sides go together.

Click a cell to place a note, click a lit cell to remove it, shift-click to accent it. Gaps are rests. On the bridge tab, pitches are placeholders: the player chooses them in the game, and only the rhythm is saved.

Sample bridge (for testing the full chain)

Pick test pitches, one per bridge slot, then hit “Intro + sample bridge + outro”. This is never saved — it stands in for what a player will choose in the game.
