Layer 1 Principles: The Foundation
1. A sentence consists of exactly 3 positions.
2. Position 1 is locked. The table experiments by placing cards in Positions 2 and 3.
3. The Knower (computer) generates a grammar from Features (Suit, Parity, Size) and Rules (e.g. “Pos 1 & 3 Match”).
4. Players take turns asking the Knower. The Analyst proposes the grammar; all Verifiers must approve before it is graded.
5. A failed round costs everyone a life. Three lives for the table — use them well.
Solo table — you are the Knower, the Analyst and the only Verifier. Nothing is hidden from you that a guest wouldn't also see.
