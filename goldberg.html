HOW TO BUILD

Press a part button, then press-and-drag on the stage — the drag sets the part's length and angle. A plain tap drops a default-size part. New parts stay put so your build holds together; drag the little end dots to reshape, the ring to turn, the middle to move.
PlacePress a part, drag on the stage (tap = default size)
SelectTap / click a part
ReshapeDrag the round end dots (balls: the edge dot)
Anchor / pivotDrag the ✛ dot (pendulum anchor, lever pivot)
RotateDrag the ring, or ⟲ / ⟳, or R (Shift+R = other way)
Lock in placeTap the part twice, then 🔒 — or L (new parts start locked)
DeleteTap twice, then 🗑 — or Erase mode, or the Del key
FlipTap twice, then ⇄ (conveyor / turntable / fan)
LinkTap twice, then 🔗, then tap another part — they move as one (🔓 splits them)
Copy / paste📋 copies the selection with its weld group, 📌 pastes at the cursor (Ctrl+C / Ctrl+V)
Spawn pointDrag the 📍 marker itself
Draw trackDraw mode, then sketch with your finger or mouse
