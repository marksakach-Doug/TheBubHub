Turn 1 / 12 You Speak

The Word Key

52 cards across 4 kinds of word · Ranks are labels only

Humus — Rules of Play

A game of words that teach each other

Worlds: A world is a sheet of paper with a growing list of words and the lines that taught them. Every world begins with four seed words drawn from the deck.

Each Turn (12 turns total):

  1. Draw 4 cards silently from the 52-card deck.
  2. Pick the Secret: Choose 1 card as the secret word; the other 3 are decoys.
  3. Pick the World: Choose an existing sheet, or start a new world (seeded with 4 fresh cards). The secret word cannot already be in or struck from that world. Any decoy already in or struck from that world is swapped for a fresh draw.
  4. Write the Line: Up to 7 words teaching the secret word. You may ONLY use words in that world (with optional -s, -es, -ed, -ing, -ly endings), free words (the, a, of, in, and, is, to, not), and . or ?. You cannot use the secret word or struck words.
  5. Decode: The 4 cards are laid out face up. The decoder points to the word they think the line was teaching.
  6. The World Keeps What Was Heard: The guessed word always joins the world. If a decoy was guessed instead of the secret, the secret word is struck through and never learned by that world.

Rules That Protect the Game

  • Never explain a line, even after a miss. Meaning stays in the players’ heads.
  • Nobody judges the poetry. A line only has to be understood.
  • There is no score. A miss isn’t a loss—it’s how the world grows.

Table & Deck Settings

Variant: A deck that points somewhere

Swap ♥Q forget → release and ♦K dry → free

The standard deck holds a lot of impermanence (ash, tide, fall) and stress (hungry, bitter, hollow); these two swaps give worlds a direction to drift toward.
