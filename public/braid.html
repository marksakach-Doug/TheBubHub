Braid

A word game of linked strands.

Waiting for the host to start.
    How to play

    Players take turns adding one word. Each new word must link to the last two words (loose) or the last three (tight), and every link is a named relation. Each turn the game deals a hand of relations. Pick a different one for each link. A hand always holds relations that fit any word, so you are never stuck, and a word never gets the same relation twice. Switch to free to choose from all eight.

    Anyone may Challenge the last word and make its author name every link out loud. A broken link drops the word. Call Home when the last word ties back to the first. Words you keep are your score.

    At a networked table

    A challenge or a Home call goes to a table vote. A challenged link holds on a tie. A Home call needs a clear majority to close the braid.

    Solo

    One voice holds two strands. You begin from a seed word and build alone, checking your own links. Your score is the words you kept, minus two for every word you drop.
