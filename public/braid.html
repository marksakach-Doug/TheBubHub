    How to play

    One stitch is three words. Players take turns adding one word at a time. Once three words are on the board, choose the two that have a clear association and explain the connection. The third word is the loose strand.

    Carry the loose word forward. It becomes the first word of the next stitch. Players add two new words, then identify a linked pair again. The loose word can remain loose for several stitches; the aim is to weave it in naturally, not force a connection.

    With three players, each person adds one word per stitch. With two players, alternate turns until the stitch has three words. With more players, continue around the circle. Keep going for as long as the group likes.

    Example: moon — tide — violin. Moon and tide are linked because the moon's gravity influences tides. Violin is the loose strand.
