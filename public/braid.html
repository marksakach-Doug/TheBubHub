Players

    How to play

    1. Start the strings. Each player adds one seed word. That is the only word they contribute during that opening turn.

    2. Choose A and B. On your turn, click two existing words on different players' strings. A is the synonym target; B is the antonym target.

    3. Add one word. Write exactly one word that you think is a synonym of A and an antonym of B. No sentences or explanations.

    4. Check the two links. Talk it through together and mark Yes or No for each relationship. A solid line means the link holds; a broken line with an × means it does not.

    Each new word is added to its author's colored string, while its two meaning-links cross to A and B. Over time, the strings and cross-links form the braid. A word can make both links, just one, or neither.

    Example: A = happy, B = sad. A possible bridge is glad: it is close in meaning to happy and opposite in meaning to sad.

    Keep it light: the group decides what counts as a convincing synonym or antonym. If a word is debatable, discuss it; the game is about the reasoning, not a dictionary ruling.
