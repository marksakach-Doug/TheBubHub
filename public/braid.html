The braid

Players

    How to play

    1. Seed the three strands. The first three turns place one starting word on the Left, Middle, and Right strands.

    2. Cross over the middle. Strands alternate crossing from the Left and Right over the Middle strand.

    3. Pivot the meaning. Enter one word that is associated with the middle strand's word and clearly NOT associated with the loose outer strand's word.

    4. Weave the braid. If the group agrees both hold, the outer strand crosses into the middle; otherwise the word is discarded and the next player tries the same crossing.
