The braid

Players

    How to play

    1. Seed two strands. The first two turns place one starting word on the Middle and Right strands.

    2. Cross and pivot. The active outer strand crosses over the middle: enter one word associated with the middle word and clearly NOT associated with the loose outer word.

    3. Other players vote. Your clock pauses while the other players tap Yes or No on both links. If rejected, your clock resumes and you must try again.

    4. Beat the 60s clock. If your timer hits zero, you are knocked out and the next player takes the turn. Last player standing wins.
